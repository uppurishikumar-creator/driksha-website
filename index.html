QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Don't see your question? Ask us — we reply quickly.

How long does a website take?

A simple business website is usually ready in 1–2 weeks. Larger stores or custom features take longer — we give you a clear timeline before we start.

Can you update my existing website?

Yes. We can redesign it, speed it up, fix mobile issues and improve SEO — or rebuild it if that is the better option. We start with a free audit.

Do you handle domain and hosting?

Yes. We set up your domain, hosting, business email and SSL, and can maintain everything for you every month.

Do you create content in Telugu?

Yes — reels, posts and website content in Telugu, English, or both, depending on who your customers are.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. Tell us about your project and we'll send a clear quote with no hidden charges.
